Stamp 1G Processing Dates - Ireland

Irish Visa Decision Tracker • Unofficial, not immigration advice

This page records the Stamp 1G processing queue for Ireland: the application date that ISD (Immigration Service Delivery) is currently processing, the lag in days and weeks, and how that lag has moved over time. Figures come from the processing dates published by ISD.

Observation of: 7 November 2026 · Data status: OK · Source: ISD-published processing dates

Latest Stamp 1G processing observation

ISD is currently processing Stamp 1G applications received on 28 September 2026 - a lag of 40 days from the observation recorded on 7 November 2026.

Dates can move when ISD publishes an update. Check the current official date before relying on a queue.

When should I submit my renewal?

ISD works in date order and publishes the date it has reached for each stamp. Under the standard rule, if you apply before your IRP expires you may remain on your existing conditions for up to 12 weeks (84 days) after expiry. Use the planner to see the current queue, the latest sensible submission date, and the end of that grace period.

Not immigration advice. Check the current ISD rule before relying on a date.

How to read the lag

  • Processing date - the application date ISD says it has reached. Applications received after that date have not been reached yet.
  • Lag - the gap between the processing date and the date ISD published it. It describes the queue, not your case.
  • Moving the wrong way - if the lag grows between observations, the queue is falling further behind; if it shrinks, ISD is catching up.

The published date covers the scheme as a whole, not your individual application.

Official sources

For the authoritative figures, use the official pages:

  • ISD visa decisions and processing times - the official published dates, updated by ISD.
  • ISD visa offices - which office handles which countries and applications.
  • Embassy of Ireland in India - contact details for the embassy that publishes the New Delhi decision lists.

If a figure here disagrees with the official page, the official page is correct - please report the difference.
