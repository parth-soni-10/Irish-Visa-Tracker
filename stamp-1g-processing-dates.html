When should I submit my renewal?

ISD works in date order and publishes the date it has reached for each stamp. Under the standard rule, if you apply before your IRP expires you may remain on your existing conditions for up to 12 weeks (84 days) after expiry. Use the planner to see the current queue, the latest sensible submission date, and the end of that grace period.

Not immigration advice. Confirm the current rule on the official ISD renewal page before relying on a date.

How to read the lag

  • Processing date - the application date ISD says it has reached. Applications received after that date have not been reached yet.
  • Lag - the gap between the processing date and the date ISD published it. It describes the queue, not your case.
  • Moving the wrong way - if the lag grows between observations, the queue is falling further behind; if it shrinks, ISD is catching up.

The queue date is published for the scheme as a whole. It is not a promise about when an individual application will be decided, and an application can be decided earlier or later than the published date suggests.
