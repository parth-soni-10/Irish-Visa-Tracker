Date archive

Irish visa decisions by date

Weekdays only • At least 10 published decisions • Six offices

One page per weekday where the six offices' public files contain at least 10 decisions. Irish visa decisions by date is the archive view: it answers what was published on a given day, which offices published it, and how many of those decisions were approved or refused. Each date page links to the exact report page and the office's official file, so a count can be checked at source. Weekends and days with no publication are deliberately not generated; a date is the report's own publication date, not the date an individual application was decided. Use the month sections below to move from a date to the offices that published that day, or open an office page for its full report history and latest file.

Why some dates are missing

A date page is only published for a weekday with at least 10 decisions in the archived reports. That prevents thousands of thin pages for weekends, bank holidays and days when an office did not publish. The absence of a date page is not a statement about any application.
