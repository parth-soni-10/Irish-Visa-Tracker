Refund policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This site does not sell anything and does not take payments, so there is nothing to refund today.

No paid products

Searching decisions, the watchlist, email alerts and browser notifications are free. There is no checkout, subscription, seat pack or paid guide on this site.

If that changes

If a paid feature is introduced, the price, the cancellation terms and the refund window will be shown before any payment is accepted, and this page will be updated first. No payment will be taken under a different policy.

Donations

The project may link to a voluntary support page. A donation is not a purchase and is not refundable as a product, but contact us if a payment was made in error and we will look at it.

Contact

Billing or refund questions: use the contact page.
