Employment Permit Processing Dates - Ireland

Irish Visa Decision Tracker • Updated from the tracker's daily scraper run • Unofficial - not immigration advice

Each published employment-permit category has its own queue. This page records the application date DETE (Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment) has reached in every category it publishes, the lag in days and weeks, and how each queue has moved over time. Figures come from the processing dates DETE publishes and are refreshed by the tracker's scheduled scraper.

How to read a processing date

  • Processing date - the application date DETE says it has reached in that category. Applications received after that date have not been reached yet.
  • Lag - the gap between the processing date and the date the figure was published. It describes the queue, not your case.
  • Fast and slow categories coexist - in the observation above, the fastest and slowest categories are months apart. A permit can be quick or slow depending on which queue it joined.
  • Direction matters more than one number - a lag that grows between observations means the queue is falling further behind; one that shrinks means it is catching up.

The published date covers the category as a whole. It is not a promise about when an individual application will be decided, and a case can be decided earlier or later than the published date suggests.

What this is not

This is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD, DETE or any Irish government body. It does not give immigration advice, cannot speed up an application, and cannot guarantee an outcome. For a decision on your own case, contact ISD or the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi.
