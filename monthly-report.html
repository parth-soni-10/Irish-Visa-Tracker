Story package: November 2026 to date

One finding, one paragraph, the method and the files. Use it in a student paper, a data-journalism class or a newsroom; attribution and a link are the only conditions.

November 2026 to date: 1,815 dated decisions, 74.5% approved. Published volume rose by 1 against October 2026.

In November 2026 to date, the official Irish visa decision files recorded 1,815 dated decisions: 1,352 approved (74.5%) and 463 refused. That is 1 more dated decisions than October 2026. Abu Dhabi accounted for the largest published share, with 947 dated decisions. The figures count decisions published in official files, not applications received, and they do not describe any individual application.

Method. Counts come from dated official reports: Abu Dhabi, Abuja and Ankara publish reporting periods, New Delhi's cumulative file is tracked by observation date and excluded from dated totals, and Beijing and Dublin publish snapshots shown separately. A correction by an office can change a month after publication. Full method: /about#method. Corrections: correction ledger.

What these dates mean
Report date
The date printed on the office file, or the end of its reporting period. It is not necessarily the date an individual decision was made.
Official data through
The newest decision date inside the latest official file. The decision count belongs to this file only.
Tracker observation
A decision row seen by the tracker outside the latest official file. It is shown separately and is not part of the official count.
Last successful check
The most recent check that successfully read the office file. A later failed attempt does not replace this timestamp.
Last attempt
The most recent scheduled run for the office, whether it succeeded or failed.
Expected next file
Calculated from the latest report date and the office's usual publication rhythm. It is an expectation, not a guarantee.
