What Dublin publishes

Dublin publishes a decision list every Tuesday and a separate queue of processing dates by visa category. The decision list is a snapshot of the latest published table, not a full dated archive on this site.

The Dublin queue is not a decision list. It shows the application date the office has reached in each category; it does not say whether an individual application has been decided.

Dublin also decides applications transferred from overseas offices, so a number that is missing from an embassy list may still appear here.

Which applications are handled by Dublin

Dublin handles applications transferred from overseas offices and certain categories, including EU Free Movement family applications; Join Family applications for an Irish national de facto partner, a CSEP/HA de facto partner or a UK national; Minister of Religion; and Volunteer.

Dublin also publishes the queue for visa categories processed by the Dublin Visa Office. The queue is shown on processing times, not as an individual decision.

A VFS centre is not the visa office. Your receipt or acknowledgement shows which office has your file.

Tuesday decisions and the Dublin queue

Dublin publishes visa decisions every Tuesday. A decision made after the latest snapshot may not appear until the next Tuesday publication.

The Dublin queue covers 13 visa categories. It shows the processing date reached, not a decision for a particular application. Use processing times for the queue and this page for the published decision list.

If your number is missing from the latest snapshot, check the official Dublin page on the next Tuesday before treating it as unpublished.

Why your number may be missing

  • The decision has not been made or published yet. The office publishes on its own schedule; a recent decision may not appear in the latest file.
  • Your file may be with another office. A VFS centre is not necessarily the visa office that decided the case.
  • The number may be in a different format. Use the 8-digit application number; an IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional.
  • You may be using a different reference. A VFS reference, passport number, payment reference or courier tracking number will not match a published decision.
  • The latest file may not have loaded. Check Data status for the last successful check. Do not read a missing file as a refusal.
  • Dublin publishes on Tuesdays. A decision made after the latest snapshot may appear in next week’s publication.

A missing number is not a refusal. It means the tracker could not find it in the reports it checked.

What Approved and Refused mean

Approved means the published report records an approval. It is not the visa itself, and it does not tell you when your passport will be returned.

Refused means the published report records a refusal. The report does not give the reason. Read your decision letter for the reason and any review or appeal deadline.

Always confirm the outcome on the official source and in your own decision letter. The tracker cannot change or appeal a decision.

How to search and get alerts

  1. Open Search with Dublin already selected.
  2. Enter the 8-digit application number.
  3. Confirm the result against the official Dublin file.

Frequently asked questions

How often does Dublin publish visa decisions?

The Dublin Visa Office publishes visa decisions every Tuesday.

Which applications does the Dublin visa office handle?

Dublin handles applications transferred from overseas offices and certain categories, including EU Free Movement family applications, specified Join Family categories, Minister of Religion and Volunteer. Check your receipt and the official ISD page for your file.

Why is my number missing from the Dublin list?

The decision may not be published until the next Tuesday, your file may be with another office, or the number may be in a different format. A missing number is not a refusal.

What is the Dublin queue?

It is a set of processing dates by visa category, not an individual decision list. See the processing times page for the current queue.

Does an Approved result mean my passport is ready?

No. The list records the decision, not the passport’s location. Wait for the official decision letter and the VFS return process.
