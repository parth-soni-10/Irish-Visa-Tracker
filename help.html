How to check your Irish visa application status

Independent, unofficial record of published decisions • Not immigration advice

Open the search with all six offices selected, enter the 8-digit application number from your receipt, and check the result against the official source. This page explains how to track an application, what the result means, why a number can be missing, and how alerts and office coverage work.

How do I check my Irish visa application status?

Open Search, leave the office selector on All six offices, and enter the application number from your receipt or acknowledgement email. It is usually an 8-digit number; an IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional. The tracker searches the published decision files only. It cannot see an application that has not been published yet.

Fastest route: open the Search tab and enter the number. If the result is missing, it is not a refusal - check the official office file and your own decision letter.

See today's published decisions by office for the newest file date and counts before you search.

Where do I find my application number?

Use the application number on your receipt, acknowledgement email or decision email. It is usually an 8-digit number; the tracker accepts 6–12 digits. An IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional.

Do not use a VFS reference number, a passport number, a payment reference or a courier tracking number. Those are different identifiers and will not match a published decision.

How do I track an Irish visa application?

There is no single official tracker for every Irish visa. Where you can see progress depends on who has your file: the visa application centre that took your documents, the visa office that decides the case, or the office's published decision list.

  1. Use the reference from your application centre. If you applied through VFS Global, the centre that accepted your documents runs its own tracking service. Use the reference on your VFS receipt on the VFS Global site, or open the tracker's VFS tracking page for applications submitted in India. VFS handles appointments, document submission and passport return; it does not decide the application.
  2. Confirm which visa office has your file. Your receipt, acknowledgement email or application summary names the office. A VFS centre is not the visa office, and some categories are transferred to Dublin.
  3. Search the office's published decisions. Search all six offices here, or open the office page for its latest file and publication schedule: New Delhi, Beijing, Abu Dhabi, Abuja, Ankara and Dublin.

A tracking reference showing "in progress" and a decision list showing no row can both be correct. This tracker records published decisions only; it cannot see the internal status of an application or tell you when a passport will be returned.

What do Approved and Refused mean?

  • Approved means the office's published report records this application as approved. It is not your visa, and it does not tell you when your passport will be returned.
  • Refused means the published report records a refusal. The report usually does not give a reason. Read your decision letter or refusal letter for the reason and any review or appeal deadline.
  • Not found means the number is not in the reports the tracker could check. It is not a refusal, and it is not proof that no decision exists.

Always confirm the outcome with the official source linked on the result card and with your own decision letter. A published row is a record, not a guarantee or legal advice.

Why might my result be missing?

  • It may not be published yet. Offices publish on different schedules, and a recent decision may not appear in the latest file.
  • It may be under another office. Search all six offices, or choose the office shown on your receipt. A VFS centre is not necessarily the visa office that decided the case.
  • The number may be in a different format. Use the digits from the application number; an IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional.
  • The office may publish a snapshot. Beijing and Dublin currently provide a latest snapshot rather than a full dated history.
  • A report may be unavailable. If a file cannot be loaded, the tracker says which office could not be checked. Do not read that as a refusal.

What is the difference between decision date and report date?

Decision date is the date attached to an individual row. Report date is the date of the office file that published that row. They can differ, especially for weekly reports. The result card shows both, plus when the tracker last checked the office data.

Where can I find the VFS centre or transit rules?

This tracker records published decisions; it does not list visa application centre addresses or book appointments. Use the official services below for those questions.

  • VFS Global — find your Irish visa application centre for appointments, document submission, passport collection and centre-specific service fees. VFS administers the centre; it does not decide the application.
  • Immigration Service Delivery — transit and transfer visa advice for whether you need a transit visa, airside versus border-control transit, and the application steps.
  • Immigration Service Delivery — visa decisions for the official published decision totals.

Visa requirements and VAC procedures can change. Confirm the current rule with VFS Global or the Irish Immigration Service before booking travel; this page is not immigration advice.

Is it safe to share a result link?

A copied result link contains the application number so the recipient can see the same card. Treat that link like the application number itself: share it only with someone you trust, and do not post it in a public group or social media.

The tracker does not put the application number in analytics events. Your browser history, clipboard and any chat app you paste the link into may still keep a copy; clear them if that matters to you.

What does each office publish?

  • New Delhi: daily published decisions, with dated history.
  • Beijing and Dublin: latest published snapshot.
  • Abu Dhabi, Abuja and Ankara: dated weekly reports.

The search panel repeats this coverage beside the office selector so a missing result does not create false confidence. See Data status for the latest report dates and ingestion state.

How do Telegram and browser alerts work?

  • Telegram: add an application number in the Alerts tab, then open the Telegram bot link. The bot sends one message when the number appears in a later published report.
  • Browser push: save an application in Saved, enable notifications for that browser, and allow notifications when prompted. Push is per browser and per application.
  • Telegram digest: if you track several applications in the Telegram bot, send /digest weekly or /digest daily. Add an hour and IANA timezone to choose delivery, for example /digest daily 8 Europe/Dublin. One message groups applications by office and says which ones have no change. /digest status shows the schedule and the last actual delivery; /digest preview shows what the next message would contain without changing the schedule, including when a daily no-change digest would be skipped. Only applications linked to the Telegram bot are included; applications saved only in this browser stay local and use browser push instead. Use /immediate 12345678 to keep one application immediate while the others are in the digest, /digest 12345678 to return that one application to the digest, and /remove 12345678 to stop alerts for one application.
  • Management: Saved shows what this browser remembers. Telegram subscriptions and digest frequency are managed inside the Telegram bot; this site does not receive a server-side list of your Telegram chats.

Alerts only work after an office publishes a decision. They cannot see an unpublished case and cannot speed up an application. The tracker will never ask for payment, OTPs or passport copies. Verify a decision on the official office page before acting on it.

Can I print or save the document checklist?

Yes. Open Tools → Document checklist, generate a checklist, tick the documents you have, and choose Print / save as PDF. Your browser's print dialog can save it as a PDF or send it to a printer.

Important limits

This tracker is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD, DETE or VFS Global, and it is not immigration advice. It cannot guarantee an outcome, change a decision, book a VFS appointment or tell you when your passport will be returned. Use the official body handling your application for a decision on your own case.

Corrections welcome. If a published row is wrong or missing, use the structured data error report with the office, report date and source URL. Recorded file differences are listed in the public correction ledger.
