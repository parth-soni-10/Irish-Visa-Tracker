Prediction guardrail

The expected-date estimator is a separate prototype with a zero-sample gate. It is not part of the decision lookup, is not presented as advice, and must not be treated as an official processing time. The source-linked processing-times page is the place to check published queues.

Crawl and indexing notes

The canonical index is /sitemap.xml; robots.txt allows the whole public site. Report permalinks, monthly archives and date pages are also linked from their office pages and from this hub.

Requesting indexing is a signed-in Search Console action under URL Inspection. The Search Console API can inspect a URL but cannot press “Request indexing”; this site does not pretend otherwise. IndexNow submissions cover Bing and other participating engines and do not replace Google's sitemap or Search Console path.
