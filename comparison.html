Competitor comparison

Compare Irish visa trackers

Public features checked 10 November 2026 • Independent and unofficial • Not immigration advice

There is more than one independent way to check published Irish visa decisions. This page compares what this tracker, VisaRelay and Snifox / Visastat publicly offer, alongside the official sources everything ultimately depends on.

The short version

Every independent tracker on this page reads the same public decision files. The useful question is not which one is “most accurate” — it is which job you need done.

  • Choose this tracker if you want a no-account, no-search-quota record across six offices, combined with Dublin queue dates, a submit-by planner, Stamp 1G and employment-permit planning.
  • Choose VisaRelay if you want application-specific email alerts or an agency dashboard; its public pages describe free checking with optional paid monitoring.
  • Choose Snifox / Visastat if you want a six-office tracker with email alerts and a broader set of visa tools; its public pages describe 10 free searches a month per number.
  • Use the official sources to confirm your own decision. No independent tracker decides, predicts or can speed up an application.

Where this tracker is a better fit

  • You want the queue, not just the answer. Decision search is paired with official processing dates and planners, so a result can inform the next application rather than only confirming the last one.
  • You do not want a search quota. There is no per-application monthly cap on searches here.
  • You want to verify the data. Source files are public, and corrections are checked against the official lists.
  • You do not want another account. Search and the planning pages work without one.

What this comparison does not claim

This is a feature comparison, not an accuracy or speed ranking. Every independent tracker here republishes public files, so none can confirm an individual decision or guarantee a queue date.
