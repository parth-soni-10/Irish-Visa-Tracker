Data study

What the published Irish visa decisions show

2026 edition • 62,920 decisions in the latest official files • Independent and unofficial

Data freshness: six offices — latest publications dated 1 November 2026 to 24 November 2026 · offices last successfully checked 18 November 2026 to 24 November 2026 · New Delhi official decisions through 23 November 2026 · tracker observed through 24 November 2026 (not in the official file) · 2 offices need attention: Beijing, Abuja. All six offices.

Six Irish visa offices publish decision files, each in a different shape: New Delhi a cumulative daily spreadsheet, three embassies weekly PDF reports, Dublin a Tuesday snapshot and Beijing an irregular cumulative file. This study measures what those files actually contain — published decisions, not the probability that any one application will be approved.

This study uses the same six-office files as the tracker. For the live New Delhi count and checked time, see Data status; to query the underlying rows, use History.

Approval rates by office

The approval share is the number of approved rows divided by all decided rows in the latest file the office published. It describes the file, not the applicant. The offices publish different things: New Delhi's file is cumulative, so its rate covers every decision in the file, while Abu Dhabi's latest PDF covers one reporting period.

Closures and seasonal reading

The tracker records 19 Irish public-holiday closure dates for 2026. Embassies also observe local holidays, so a missing file on a working day in Ireland can still be a closure abroad. Treat the monthly series as publication volume, not demand: a short month can mean a holiday, a missing report or a publication gap.

There is not enough complete-year history to claim a stable seasonal pattern. The honest finding is coverage: publication is continuous for the dated offices where reports exist, with visible gaps where a report was not available.

Method and limits

  • Latest-file counts come from each office's most recent published file, as recorded in data_manifest.json.
  • Dated counts come from the dated rows in the office JSON files, plus New Delhi file growth between archived file dates in the report archive.
  • Approval rate is approved rows divided by approved plus refused rows. The files do not include visa category, nationality or refusal reason.
  • No personal data is used. Application numbers are searchable on the tracker but are not included in this study's aggregates.
  • Not predictive. Nothing here estimates an individual application's chance of approval or its processing time.

What these dates mean
Report date
The date printed on the office file, or the end of its reporting period. It is not necessarily the date an individual decision was made.
Official data through
The newest decision date inside the latest official file. The decision count belongs to this file only.
Tracker observation
A decision row seen by the tracker outside the latest official file. It is shown separately and is not part of the official count.
Last successful check
The most recent check that successfully read the office file. A later failed attempt does not replace this timestamp.
Last attempt
The most recent scheduled run for the office, whether it succeeded or failed.
Expected next file
Calculated from the latest report date and the office's usual publication rhythm. It is an expectation, not a guarantee.
