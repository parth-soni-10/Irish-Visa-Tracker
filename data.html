Definitions

  • Published decision. One application number and the outcome printed in an official file: Approved or Refused. It is not a prediction and not a legal status check.
  • Report date. The date on the official file or the end of its reporting period. It is not necessarily the date an individual decision was made, and each office has its own report date.
  • Approval share. Approved decisions divided by all decisions in the same file or period. It describes published rows, not the chance that a particular application is approved.
  • Dated decision. A row whose office publishes an official decision or report-period date. New Delhi tracker rows are observation-dated and excluded from dated monthly totals; New Delhi, Beijing and Dublin latest files are reported separately.
  • Missing row. A number absent from a file has not necessarily been refused. It may be unpublished, handled by another office, or affected by a file gap or OCR error.

Coverage and freshness

Coverage starts with the oldest report currently archived (4 January 2026). The newest office publication is dated 24 November 2026, but that is not a shared cutoff. For example, New Delhi's latest file is dated 23 November 2026 and its official decisions are dated through 23 November 2026; the tracker has observed decisions dated through 24 November 2026 in a partial day not in that file. The six offices publish on different schedules: New Delhi on business days, Abu Dhabi, Abuja and Ankara weekly, Dublin on Tuesdays and Beijing irregularly. A missing office report is a gap, not a zero. Read the manifest for every office's report date and freshness.json for its official data-through date and any trailing tracker observation.

The data is refreshed by a scheduled scraper and committed to the public repository. GitHub Actions can run late; offices do not publish on weekends or public holidays. Read lastRunAt and ok in visa_meta.json before describing the data as current.

Reuse and citation

The underlying decisions are the publications of the Irish embassies and the Immigration Service Delivery and remain subject to their own re-use terms. The tracker’s compilation, charts and tables may be reused with attribution to the Irish Visa Decision Tracker and a link to this page. The source code is MIT licensed.

What these dates mean
Report date
The date printed on the office file, or the end of its reporting period. It is not necessarily the date an individual decision was made.
Official data through
The newest decision date inside the latest official file. The decision count belongs to this file only.
Tracker observation
A decision row seen by the tracker outside the latest official file. It is shown separately and is not part of the official count.
Last successful check
The most recent check that successfully read the office file. A later failed attempt does not replace this timestamp.
Last attempt
The most recent scheduled run for the office, whether it succeeded or failed.
Expected next file
Calculated from the latest report date and the office's usual publication rhythm. It is an expectation, not a guarantee.
