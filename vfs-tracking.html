VFS Global handoff

Track an Irish visa application on VFS Global

Independent and unofficial · Not immigration advice

If you submitted your documents at a VFS Global centre in India, VFS runs the application tracking service. Enter the reference from your VFS receipt and the applicant's last name to open the official tracking page. This site does not receive or store either value.

Open the official VFS tracking page

The form opens VFS Global in a new tab and passes the two values in that page's address. They are not sent to this site. VFS shows the live status only after it verifies the reference. You can also sign in to VFS Global directly.

What you need

  • The VFS reference number. Use the reference on your VFS appointment, submission receipt or acknowledgement, not the 8-digit Irish application number. Do not add spaces.
  • The applicant's last name. Enter the surname exactly as it appears on the application.
  • The right VFS centre. This handoff is for applications submitted in India. If you applied in another country, open the VFS Global Ireland page for that country instead.

VFS tracking is not the visa decision

VFS tracks an application through its centres: the appointment, document submission and passport return. The visa office decides the application and later publishes approved and refused application numbers in its own decision file.

To see whether a decision has been published, use today's decisions by office or check application status in the published lists. A VFS reference will not match a published decision; search with the 8-digit Irish application number instead.

If the reference does not work

  • Copy the reference exactly from the VFS receipt; do not add spaces or dashes.
  • Use the surname exactly as it appears on the application.
  • Allow time for the VFS centre to activate tracking after submission.
  • Contact the VFS centre that accepted your documents. This site cannot see or change a VFS record, and it cannot tell you when a passport will be returned.
