After approval

Approved: what happens after an Irish visa decision

General information • Not legal advice • Reviewed 23 November 2026

An approval in a published decision file means the visa office granted the application. It is not the same as a residence permission, and it does not guarantee entry at the border. The decision letter and the visa in your passport are the authoritative documents.

1. Wait for the passport and the decision letter

The office or its visa application centre will tell you how to submit or collect your passport. Do not book non-refundable travel until your passport is back and you have checked the visa. A published decision can appear before the passport is returned.

Keep the decision letter. It identifies the application, the visa type and any conditions. If the letter has not arrived, contact the office or VFS centre that handled the application, not the tracker.

2. Check the visa before you travel

  • Identity details — your name and passport number must match the passport you will travel on.
  • Validity — check the “from” and “until” dates and whether the visa is single or multiple entry.
  • Conditions — the visa may be tied to a course, employer, sponsor or purpose of travel.
  • Transit — a separate transit visa may be needed if you travel through another country.

If anything is wrong, contact the issuing office before travelling. The tracker cannot change a visa or correct a decision.

3. Registration and arrival

Some visa types require registration or an appointment after arrival; others do not. The requirement depends on your permission and length of stay, so check the Immigration Service Delivery guidance for your category. A visa permits you to seek entry; the border officer decides whether you may enter.

Carry your decision letter, passport, insurance and the documents you used in the application. Do not rely on a screenshot of this tracker at the border.

Official sources

  • ISD visa decisions — what happens after an application is decided.
  • Immigration Service Delivery — permission types, registration and arrival guidance.
  • VFS Global — passport return and collection at the centre that handled the application.

This page is general information, not immigration or legal advice. If your circumstances are complex, use a qualified adviser or the official office that issued the decision.
