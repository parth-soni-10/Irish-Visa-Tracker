Office finder

Find my Irish visa office

Country + category + application date • Official links • Not a prediction

Irish visa applications are handled by different offices depending on the country and application type. This helper narrows the likely office and shows the latest published file. It does not know where your individual file is and cannot estimate your decision date.

What the public file can tell you about timing

The decision files list decisions already made. They do not publish an application-date queue for every office, so this page does not invent an expected decision date. Use the office's official processing page, the latest report date, and your receipt. The processing-times page explains the published queues and their limits.

Transfers happen: if the receipt names an office but no decision appears there, search all six offices before assuming anything. This is general information, not immigration advice.
