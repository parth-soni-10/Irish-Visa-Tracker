Irish Visa Decision Tracker

The Irish Visa Decision Tracker (also known as the Irish Visa Update Tracker) is an independent, unofficial record of visa decisions published by the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi, with daily approved and refused summaries, Stamp 1G processing dates and employment-permit queues. Search any published application number, follow trends over time, and read community-reported wait times.

What this tracker covers

  • Visa decisions — each application number in the embassy's daily spreadsheet, with its decision date and approved/refused outcome.
  • Daily summaries and trends — accepted and refused counts for every published day, with the applications behind those counts available on demand.
  • Stamp 1G and employment permits — the processing dates reached by ISD and DETE, the lag in days and weeks, and how it has moved.
  • Community timelines — waits shared by visitors and merged nightly. They are anecdotes, not a statistical sample.

Data, sources and limits

Decision rows come from the embassy's published spreadsheet. Stamp 1G and permit figures come from the processing dates published by ISD and DETE. A scheduled scraper commits new rows to the public GitHub repository, and this page reads those JSON files, so a pipeline run appears without a redeploy. Check visa_meta.json for scraper health before treating the figures as current.

This project is not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD, DETE or any Irish government body. It does not give immigration advice, predict an individual outcome or see private case data. For a decision on your own application, contact the official body handling it.
