The Irish Visa Decision Tracker (also known as the Irish Visa Update Tracker) is an independent, unofficial record of visa decisions published by six Irish visa offices, with daily approved and refused summaries, Stamp 1G processing dates and employment-permit queues. Search any published application number, follow trends over time, and read community-reported wait times.

What this tracker covers

  • Visa decisions — each application number in the published office reports, with its decision date, report date and approved/refused outcome.
  • Daily summaries and trends — approved and refused counts for every published day, with the applications behind those counts available on demand.
  • Stamp 1G and employment permits — the processing dates reached by ISD and DETE, the lag in days and weeks, and how it has moved.
  • Community timelines — waits shared by visitors and merged nightly. They are anecdotes, not a statistical sample.

Help & FAQ explains application-number formats, statuses and missing results.

Data, sources and limits

Decision rows come from the published reports of six Irish visa offices: New Delhi, Beijing, Abu Dhabi, Abuja, Ankara and Dublin. Stamp 1G and employment permit dates come from ISD and DETE. Each dashboard shows when its figures were last updated.

This project is not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD or DETE and does not provide immigration advice. For a decision on your own application, contact the official body handling it.
