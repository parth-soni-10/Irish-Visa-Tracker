Press and citation kit

Six offices • Independent and unofficial

Data freshness: six offices — latest publications dated 1 November 2026 to 21 November 2026 · offices last successfully checked 18 November 2026 to 21 November 2026 · New Delhi decisions through 20 November 2026 · 2 offices need attention: Beijing, Abuja. All six offices.

The tracker is an independent, searchable record of the decision files published by six Irish visa offices. It is useful for a story about published decision volume, approval shares or the shape of the official files — never as a prediction for an individual application.

What the tracker records

For each office the tracker stores the published application number, decision and the date basis the office uses: a daily cumulative file for New Delhi, weekly report periods for Abu Dhabi, Abuja and Ankara, and dated snapshots for Beijing and Dublin. The latest-file totals are New Delhi: 43,599; Beijing: 13,724; Abu Dhabi: 232; Abuja: 99; Ankara: 165; Dublin: 4,475.

The files do not contain visa category, nationality, refusal reason or applicant demographics. Any statement about those would be unsupported by this data.

Story angles the data supports

  • Approval shares differ sharply by office. The latest files range from a weekly report where two-thirds were refused to large cumulative files with four-fifths approved; the data study sets out the figures and caveats.
  • Publication volume is not demand. Weekly reports show how many decisions an office published in a period, not how many people applied.
  • New Delhi's cumulative file is measurable day by day. Archived file dates show how the list grew across the run of file dates in November 2026.
  • Gaps are visible. The tracker records missing reports as gaps rather than estimating them.

Cite this data

Use the study URL and the data date. The citation is stable; the figures behind it are rebuilt from the latest official files.

Parth Soni. (2026). Irish Visa Decision Tracker: published visa decisions by office (latest office publications dated 1 November 2026 to 21 November 2026; offices last successfully checked 18 November 2026 to 21 November 2026) [Data set]. https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data-study

A versioned snapshot of the summary tables and metadata is published at /data/snapshots/2026-11-21/metadata.json.

DOI-backed snapshots

A DOI is minted by depositing a dated snapshot with a repository such as Zenodo; it cannot be created by the site itself. The tracker publishes the snapshot files and citation metadata, so a deposit can point at an immutable version and the study can cite the DOI once it exists.

Publishing the DOI requires the maintainer's repository account. Until then, cite the study URL and the data date above. Corrections are logged in the correction ledger.

Interview and correction requests

Use the contact page for questions, methodology details or a correction. The tracker cannot comment on an individual application or change a decision.
