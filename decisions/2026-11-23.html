Decisions by date

Irish visa decisions published on 23 November 2026

48,275 decisions • 39,556 approved • 8,719 refused • 81.9% approved share

On 23 November 2026, 48,275 decisions across 2 offices, 81.9% approved; New Delhi led with 43,800. This page groups the official reports whose own publication date is 23 November 2026. It exists because the date is a weekday with at least 10 published decisions. Counts are published rows, not applications received; a number that is absent from one file may be in another office's file or still awaiting publication.

What this date page can and cannot tell you

  • It can show which reports are dated that day and the counts printed in them.
  • It can link to the exact report page and the office's official file.
  • It cannot tell you that your application was decided on this date, or why a decision was made.
  • It cannot stand in for your decision letter. A missing number is not a refusal.
