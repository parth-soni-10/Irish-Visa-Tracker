Rows are deduplicated by application number within each office, so a repeated application is counted at its most recent report date. Period dates are report-period end dates, not the date of an individual decision.

What the data does and does not show

  • Four offices have dated history. New Delhi, Abu Dhabi, Abuja and Ankara keep published decision rows across report dates, which is what powers the daily views and historical comparisons. Beijing and Dublin remain latest-report snapshots.
  • History coverage is not uniform. Older weekly PDFs were backfilled where available, so the archive may have gaps. A repeated application number is shown at its most recent report date.
  • Report dates are not individual decision dates. Where an office publishes a weekly or period report, the row date is the report period end. The official source remains the only place to confirm an individual case.
  • Small offices are genuinely small. Abuja and Ankara publish far fewer rows than New Delhi or Beijing. Their totals should not be compared as if they were the same population.

Freshness

The scraper runs on a schedule and commits new official files to the repository. GitHub's scheduled runs can be delayed, and offices do not publish on weekends or public holidays. A successful page load is not proof that a new report was published today; check the scraper health file and the report dates above.

Spotted a stale or incorrect row? Email soni.soni.parth@gmail.com with the office, application number and report date. Corrections are checked against the official source.
