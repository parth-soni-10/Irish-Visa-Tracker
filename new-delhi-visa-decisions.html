What New Delhi publishes

Each morning, by about 11:00 Indian Standard Time, the Embassy publishes a cumulative spreadsheet of visa decisions made in 2026 up to the previous day. Each row is an application number and one outcome: Approved or Refused.

The list is cumulative, not a daily-only file. The tracker keeps the dated history, so you can search one application number or see how many decisions were published on a given day.

New Delhi does not provide individual status updates. The published list is the status record; the embassy asks applicants not to contact it for updates.

What this file cannot tell you

  • It cannot tell you that your application was decided on a particular day, or why a decision was made. The file carries an outcome, not a reason.
  • It does not publish an application-date queue, nationality, visa category or applicant demographics.
  • A number that is absent is not a refusal. It may be unpublished, in another office’s file, or written differently on your receipt.

Approval share is approved published rows divided by published rows: it describes the file, not the chance that a particular application will be approved. Method and sources · Correction ledger · Data study

Which applications are handled by New Delhi

New Delhi accepts visa applications from India, Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka.

A VFS centre is not the visa office. Your receipt or acknowledgement shows which office has your file.

Some applications are transferred to Dublin for processing: EU Free Movement family applications; Join Family applications for an Irish national de facto partner, a CSEP/HA de facto partner or a UK national; Minister of Religion; and Volunteer. Those decisions appear in Dublin’s list, not New Delhi’s.

If your number is missing here, search all six offices before concluding anything.

Why your number may be missing

  • The decision has not been made or published yet. The office publishes on its own schedule; a recent decision may not appear in the latest file.
  • Your file may be with another office. A VFS centre is not necessarily the visa office that decided the case.
  • The number may be in a different format. Use the 8-digit application number; an IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional.
  • You may be using a different reference. A VFS reference, passport number, payment reference or courier tracking number will not match a published decision.
  • The latest file may not have loaded. Check Data status for the last successful check. Do not read a missing file as a refusal.
  • Another office may have the file. Some categories are decided in Dublin; search all six offices rather than assuming New Delhi refused or lost the application.

A missing number is not a refusal. It means the tracker could not find it in the reports it checked.

What Approved and Refused mean

Approved means the published report records an approval. It is not the visa itself, and it does not tell you when your passport will be returned.

Refused means the published report records a refusal. The report does not give the reason. Read your decision letter for the reason and any review or appeal deadline.

Always confirm the outcome on the official source and in your own decision letter. The tracker cannot change or appeal a decision.

Frequently asked questions

How often does New Delhi publish visa decisions?

Each morning, by about 11:00 Indian Standard Time, the embassy publishes a cumulative list of decisions made in 2026 up to the previous day.

Where do I find my New Delhi application number?

Use the 8-digit application number on your receipt, acknowledgement email or decision email. An IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional. Do not use a VFS reference, passport number or courier tracking number.

Which countries does the New Delhi visa office handle?

India, Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka. Some categories are processed in Dublin instead, so search all six offices if your number is missing.

Why is my application number not in the New Delhi list?

It may not be decided or published yet, your file may be with Dublin, the number may be in a different format, or the latest file may not have loaded. A missing number is not a refusal.

How long does a New Delhi visa take?

The embassy’s published usual timeframes are 4–6 weeks for Employment Category A, 8–10 weeks for Category B, 4–8 weeks for Study, 4 weeks for Business/Conference/Event, 6–8 weeks for Visit and 6–12 months for Join Family. They are not guarantees and exclude VFS transit.

Does an Approved result mean my passport is ready?

No. The list records the decision, not the passport’s location. Wait for the official decision letter and the VFS return process.

How many decisions are in the latest New Delhi file?

The file dated 24 November 2026 contains 43,937 decisions: 37,019 approved and 6,918 refused.
