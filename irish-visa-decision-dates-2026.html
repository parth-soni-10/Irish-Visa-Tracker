What the dates mean

  • Date pages group every office report whose own publication date matches the date, but only for weekdays with at least 10 decisions.
  • Dated reports link the exact report page for each office file. New Delhi’s file date is the official-file date; weekly reports use their period-end date.
  • Decision date is the date attached to a published row when the office provides one. It can differ from the report date, especially for weekly reports.
  • A missing date means no report cleared the archive threshold or no file was published. It is not evidence that an application was refused.
