# Composition Brief — Irish Visa Decision Tracker

## Story arc
Every business day the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi posts one flat spreadsheet of visa decisions — no history, no search, no trends. This tool archives the daily lists and turns them into a searchable, trackable dashboard.

## Visual language
- **Palette:** white `#ffffff` / slate `#f8fafc` scenes alternating with deep ink `#0f172a`
- **Status colors:** green `#059669` (accepted), amber `#d97706` (pending), red `#dc2626` (refused)
- **Type:** Geist (site font), heavy weights for the wordmark, tabular mono for application numbers
- **Motifs:** passport/search icon feel, application-number rows, status chips

## Scenes (30s, soft crossfades between scenes)

1. **Hook (0–7s)** — white. Eyebrow "IRISH VISA TRACKER" spaced caps; giant "VISAS," + green "tracked." + status dots (accepted / pending / refused).
2. **Proof (6.2–13.8s)** — white. "The embassy posts one spreadsheet a day. No history. No search. No answers." Pills: SEARCH · WATCHLIST · HISTORY.
3. **The Work (13–23s)** — ink. Dashboard card: search input "Search by application number…" with three result rows — Approved (green chip), Pending (amber chip), Refused (red chip) — and acceptance-rate / decisions-today cards.
4. **Philosophy (22.2–27s)** — white. Big quote mark + "The answer, the day it's posted."
5. **Outro (26.2–30s)** — ink. "IRISH VISA TRACKER" + "The answer, the day it's posted." + "New Delhi → You".
