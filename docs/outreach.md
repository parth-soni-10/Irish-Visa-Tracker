# Ethical data-story outreach

The goal is a citation or a link from a relevant student office, immigration
solicitor, researcher, student newsroom or applicant community. This is not a
directory-submission or mass-email exercise.

Sending outreach is an external action. The maintainer must approve the final
list and send from their own address; this repository only prepares the kit.

## The asset

- [The data study](https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data-study) — the
  original analysis, methodology and downloadable tables.
- [The monthly report](https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/monthly-report) —
  a citable month-by-month record.
- [The report archive](https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/reports) — dated
  official reports with checksums and source files.
- [Open data](https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data) — JSON/CSV files,
  definitions and reuse terms.
- [Press and citation kit](https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/press) —
  key statistics, citation snippet and licence terms.

Always describe the project as **independent and unofficial**. It is not the
Embassy of Ireland, ISD or DETE, and it cannot comment on an individual case.

## Who to approach

Prioritise a small number of relevant recipients:

1. International student offices and university visa advisers in Ireland.
2. Immigration solicitors and approved advisers who publish explainers.
3. Student newspapers and higher-education journalists.
4. Researchers working on migration, administrative data or public-sector
   information.
5. Moderated applicant communities that allow data posts.

Do not buy lists, scrape personal emails, or send to hundreds of addresses.
One relevant email is worth more than a hundred directory listings.

## Pitch template

> **Subject:** Independent data on Irish visa decisions (not an official source)
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I run the Irish Visa Decision Tracker, an independent, unofficial project
> that records the visa decisions published by the six Irish visa offices. It
> now holds [N] dated reports and a study of [N] published decisions.
>
> I thought it might be useful for [specific reason tied to their audience]:
> [one concrete finding, with the date and the office].
>
> The method, limits and downloadable data are here:
> https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data-study
>
> I am not asking for a link. If the data is useful, you are welcome to cite it
> with the date and a link; the citation snippet and licence terms are at
> https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/press. I am happy to answer a
> methodology question or provide the underlying JSON.
>
> Best,
> Parth Soni
> Independent project · not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD or DETE

Adapt the finding; do not send the same generic paragraph to everyone. Never
claim the data predicts an individual outcome.

## Tracking

Use campaign-tagged links so referrals are attributable without a tracking
pixel:

```
https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data-study?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=study_2026
```

Keep a simple log: recipient category, date sent, specific finding offered,
reply, link/citation, and follow-up date. Do not follow up more than once.

## Rules

- Send only where the recipient's work makes the data relevant.
- Do not misstate the sample, freshness or official status.
- Do not offer to remove or alter a published decision.
- Do not send personal data, application numbers or private case details.
- If someone asks not to be contacted again, record it and do not contact them.
- A nofollow link or a citation with no link is still useful; do not pressure
  for SEO.
