# Citation, DOI and outreach

The site publishes the durable assets; the actions below need the maintainer's
accounts and judgement.

## Deposit the monthly snapshot (Zenodo)

1. Sign in to Zenodo and create a new upload.
2. Upload the files in `data/snapshots/<YYYY-MM-DD>/`: `office-summary.csv`,
   `monthly-volume.csv`, `metadata.json`, `CITATION.cff` and `zenodo.json`.
3. Use the `zenodo.json` fields as the record metadata (title, creators,
   version, keywords, related identifiers). Add the DOI only after publishing.
4. Publish, then copy the DOI and add it to the press page citation. Do not
   invent a DOI or add one before Zenodo has reserved it.
5. Repeat for a new dated snapshot only when the underlying data changes
   materially. A daily re-upload is not a citable version.

Until a DOI exists, cite the study URL and the snapshot date:

> Parth Soni. (2026). *Irish Visa Decision Tracker: published visa decisions by
> office* (latest office publications dated ...). [Data set].
> https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/data-study

## Current status (25 November 2026)

No outreach has been sent. Sending is an external action and requires the
maintainer's approval and their own address; this repository only prepares the
kit. The lowest-friction first ask is the badge/widget at
`https://irishvisaupdatetracker.pages.dev/embed`: a university or adviser page
can show the latest file date and count with attribution without copying a
table. The second ask is a citation of `/data-study` or `/monthly-report` in a
methodology or student-support page.

Do not buy links, exchange links, or send the same note to a bulk list. A small
number of relevant placements is the goal.

## Outreach targets

Lead with a monthly finding, not the daily page: "New Delhi approval rate and
decision volume" is easier to cite than a changing snapshot. Link the data study,
report archive or monthly report and let those pages pass readers to the today
page and office pages.

- University international offices and student advisers (India, Bangladesh,
  Nepal, Sri Lanka, China, Nigeria, Türkiye).
- Immigration solicitors and regulated consultants, especially Dublin practices
  handling join-family and Pakistan transfers.
- Student communities and moderated forums where the reply answers a real
  question; do not drop links into unrelated threads.
- Journalists covering immigration, international education or Irish public
  data.
- Researchers and librarians looking for reusable immigration datasets.

Avoid paid links, link exchanges and bulk link drops. A citation from a page
that actually uses the data is worth more than dozens of directory listings.

## Track the result

Keep one row per contact: date, organisation, page linked, response, and whether
the link is live. Review quarterly; drop targets that never engage rather than
repeating the same message.
