Daily Visa Decision Summaries

Irish Visa Decision Tracker • Data as of 31 October 2026 • Unofficial — not immigration advice

Every business day the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi publishes a spreadsheet of visa decisions. This page summarises the approved and refused counts for each published day, drawn from the tracker's running record of those decisions.

Data as of: 31 October 2026 · Scraper status: OK · Total rows tracked: 41,462 · Source: /data/visa_decisions.json

How to read this

  • Approved — applications the embassy has marked as approved in the published daily spreadsheet.
  • Refused — applications the embassy has marked as refused.
  • Days with no embassy file are shown as closures or placeholders, not as decisions.

Raw data

The complete decision history is available as a public JSON file: /data/visa_decisions.json (approximately 2 MB, shaped as [date, application number, decision] rows). Scraper health is at /data/visa_meta.json.

What this is not

This is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with the Embassy of Ireland, ISD, DETE or any Irish government body. It does not give immigration advice, cannot speed up an application, and cannot guarantee an outcome. For a decision on your own case, contact ISD or the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi.
