Terms of use

Last updated 10 November 2026

The tracker is a free, independent record of publicly published Irish visa decision lists. It is not the official service, and it does not give immigration advice.

What this service is

We read public decision files published by Irish embassies and the Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), keep a history of them, and make them searchable. We are not affiliated with any Irish government body, embassy, VFS Global or AVATS.

No advice and no guarantees

A published decision is the only outcome we can show. A missing application number does not mean a refusal or an approval, and a queue date is not a promise about an individual file. Always confirm with the office handling your application before acting on anything here.

Acceptable use

Use the site for your own application or for helping others check theirs. Do not scrape at a rate that degrades the service, attempt to access another person's account, or use the data to make decisions that require legal advice. The underlying official data is re-used under the Irish re-use of public sector information rules; the tracker's own compilation and history are provided for personal and informational use.

Accounts and alerts

Accounts are optional. You are responsible for keeping your password secure. Alerts are best-effort: official publication, email delivery and browser push can all be delayed or fail. Do not rely on an alert as the only way to check a decision.

Availability and changes

The service is provided as-is, without warranties. Features and data coverage can change or stop. We may suspend an account that abuses the service. These terms may be updated; the date above is the version in force.

Contact

Questions or corrections: use the contact page. Privacy: read the privacy notice.
