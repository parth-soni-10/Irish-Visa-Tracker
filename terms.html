Terms of use

Last updated 10 November 2026

The tracker is a free, independent record of publicly published Irish visa decision lists. It is not the official service, and it does not give immigration advice.

What this service is

We read public decision files published by Irish embassies and the Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), keep a history of them, and make them searchable. We are not affiliated with any Irish government body, embassy, VFS Global or AVATS.

No advice and no guarantees

A published decision is the only outcome we can show. A missing application number does not mean a refusal or an approval, and a queue date is not a promise about an individual file. Always confirm with the office handling your application before acting on anything here.

Acceptable use

Use the site for your own application or for helping others check theirs. Do not scrape at a rate that degrades the service or use the data to make decisions that require legal advice. The underlying official data is re-used under the Irish re-use of public sector information rules and remains subject to the publishers' terms. The tracker's own compilation, charts and tables may be reused with attribution to the Irish Visa Decision Tracker and a link to the open-data page; the source code is MIT licensed.

Watchlists and alerts

Watchlists are stored in your browser; there are no accounts or passwords. Alerts are best-effort: official publication, Telegram, digest delivery and browser push can all be delayed or fail. Do not rely on an alert as the only way to check a decision.

The tracker will never ask for payment, OTPs or passport copies. A Telegram message or page that does is not from this tracker; verify the decision on the official office page.

Availability and changes

The service is provided as-is, without warranties. Features and data coverage can change or stop. We may block abusive use of the service. These terms may be updated; the date above is the version in force.

Contact

Questions or corrections: use the contact page. Privacy: read the privacy notice.
