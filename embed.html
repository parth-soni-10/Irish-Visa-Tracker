Badges inherit the site's no-store policy so a deploy's numbers are never stale. They are rebuilt from the public manifest, so the numbers always match the office page at the time of the last deploy.

What the badge says

  • Office and report date — the latest official file the tracker holds.
  • Decisions and approval share — published rows in that file, not an applicant's odds.
  • No personal data — application numbers never appear in the image or its URL.
