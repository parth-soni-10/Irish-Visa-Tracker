Community processing timelines: the aggregate view

Anonymous aggregates only • Minimum group size 10 • Anecdotes, not statistics

Visitors can share how long their own application took. Those submissions are anecdotes, not a representative sample. This page publishes a group only when it has at least 10 submissions, so no single person's timeline can be identified by subtraction.

Privacy rules

  • No individual rows. The page never lists an application, a date pair or a free-text note on its own.
  • No identifiers. Names, emails, passport numbers and application numbers are not part of the aggregate file.
  • Small groups stay hidden. A group below 10 is suppressed, including when it is the only group.
  • Anecdotes are labelled. Submission is self-selected, so the aggregate can over-represent fast or slow cases.

Source: community_timelines.json. The tracker's own processing-time tracker is separate and based on official queues.

What these dates mean
Report date
The date printed on the office file, or the end of its reporting period. It is not necessarily the date an individual decision was made.
Official data through
The newest decision date inside the latest official file. The decision count belongs to this file only.
Tracker observation
A decision row seen by the tracker outside the latest official file. It is shown separately and is not part of the official count.
Last successful check
The most recent check that successfully read the office file. A later failed attempt does not replace this timestamp.
Last attempt
The most recent scheduled run for the office, whether it succeeded or failed.
Expected next file
Calculated from the latest report date and the office's usual publication rhythm. It is an expectation, not a guarantee.
