What Ankara publishes

Ankara publishes a weekly PDF decision report. Each report covers a period and lists the applications decided in that period, with an approved or refused outcome.

Some Ankara reports are image-based PDFs. The tracker extracts their text with OCR at 300 DPI and stores the result as a searchable report, so a name or number can be misread at the source.

The official PDF is always the authority. If a row looks wrong, check the source file before relying on it.

Which applications are handled by Ankara

Ankara accepts visa applications from Türkiye, Azerbaijan and visa-required nationals resident in Saudi Arabia.

A VFS centre is not the visa office. Your receipt or acknowledgement shows which office has your file.

If your number is missing here, search all six offices before concluding anything.

How the weekly reports and OCR work

Each report covers a reporting period and ends on the report date shown above. The applications in that report may have been decided at different points inside the period.

Ankara reports can be scanned documents. The tracker uses poppler and Tesseract OCR at 300 DPI to make them searchable. OCR can miss or misread a character, so the extracted row is a finding aid, not a certified copy.

The archive currently holds 5 weekly reports. A missing number may be in an earlier or later report, so use the office history and check the official page for the newest file.

Why your number may be missing

  • The decision has not been made or published yet. The office publishes on its own schedule; a recent decision may not appear in the latest file.
  • Your file may be with another office. A VFS centre is not necessarily the visa office that decided the case.
  • The number may be in a different format. Use the 8-digit application number; an IRL prefix, spaces and dashes are optional.
  • You may be using a different reference. A VFS reference, passport number, payment reference or courier tracking number will not match a published decision.
  • The latest file may not have loaded. Check Data status for the last successful check. Do not read a missing file as a refusal.
  • OCR may have missed the row. Check the official PDF for the report period before treating a missing number as unpublished.

A missing number is not a refusal. It means the tracker could not find it in the reports it checked.

What Approved and Refused mean

Approved means the published report records an approval. It is not the visa itself, and it does not tell you when your passport will be returned.

Refused means the published report records a refusal. The report does not give the reason. Read your decision letter for the reason and any review or appeal deadline.

Always confirm the outcome on the official source and in your own decision letter. The tracker cannot change or appeal a decision.

Frequently asked questions

How often does Ankara publish visa decisions?

The office publishes weekly PDF decision reports. Each report covers a period and carries the period-end date.

Which countries does the Ankara visa office handle?

Türkiye, Azerbaijan and visa-required nationals resident in Saudi Arabia.

Why is my number missing from the Ankara list?

It may be in an earlier or later report, the decision may not be published yet, OCR may have missed the row, or your file may be with another office. Check the official PDF before treating it as a refusal.

Why does Ankara data have an OCR note?

Some reports are scanned images. The tracker extracts their text with OCR to make them searchable; the official PDF is the authority.

Does an Approved result mean my passport is ready?

No. The report records the decision, not the passport’s location. Wait for the official decision letter and the VFS return process.
